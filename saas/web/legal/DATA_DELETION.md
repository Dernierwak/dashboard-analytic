# Data Deletion Instructions / Suppression de tes données

_Last updated: <DATE>_

This page explains how to have your data deleted from **Pulse**, operated by **<COMPANY_NAME>**.

**Pulse has no self-service delete button.** Deletion is handled manually by us, on request. We say so here, and in our [Privacy Policy](/privacy) §6, rather than promising a button that does not exist.

---

## 1. Stop the collection yourself, immediately

You do not need us for this, and it takes one click.

In **Mes comptes**, use **Déconnecter** on either connection:
- **Google** — covers Google Ads and Google Analytics 4
- **Meta** — covers Meta Ads and Instagram

This **deletes the credentials we hold** for that platform, so no further data can be fetched and you would have to authorize us again to resume. Said precisely: it erases our copy of the token, it does not call Google's or Meta's revocation endpoint.

To revoke the authorization **at the source**, which is the stronger action, do it on the platform itself:
- Google — [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions)
- Meta — Settings → Business Integrations

Neither of these deletes the data already stored. That is what the request below is for.

---

## 2. Ask for your data to be deleted

Write to **<PRIVACY_EMAIL>** from the email address of your Pulse account, with the subject **"Suppression de mes données"** / **"Data deletion request"**.

Say which of the two you want:

- **Delete one connected source** — we delete the credentials and every figure fetched from that platform.
- **Delete the whole account** — we delete everything listed in §3.

We answer within **30 days**, and we tell you when it is done. We may ask you to confirm from the account email address before acting; that is the only check we perform, and it exists so that nobody else can delete your account.

If you have no access to the account email address any more, write to us from any address and give the account email plus the connected ad account identifiers: we will find another way to establish that the account is yours.

---

## 3. What gets deleted

- Your **account** and its authentication record
- Every **OAuth token** and platform identifier we hold for you (Google Ads, GA4, Meta Ads, Facebook Page, Instagram Business Account)
- Every **figure fetched** from those platforms: campaign, ad, analytics and Instagram post rows
- Everything **you wrote in Pulse**: themes, priorities, objectives, notes, tracked actions and the feedback you gave on recommendations
- Every **weekly report** published to your account

Deletion is permanent and we keep no backup copy of it beyond the deletion window. We keep nothing except what the law requires us to keep (accounting records, where applicable).

We hold **no copy of your data at Google or Meta**: our access is read-only, and deleting your Pulse data does not touch a single thing in your Google Ads, Google Analytics, Meta Ads or Instagram accounts. Those stay exactly as they are.
